Senior Product Designer — Northwind Cloud

About the role

We're hiring a Senior Product Designer to lead design for our developer tools surface — CLI, dashboard, and the integrations marketplace. You'll be the second designer on the team and will work closely with our PM, engineering leads, and DevRel.

What you'll do

- Own design for the developer-facing surface end-to-end: research, exploration, system, ship.
- Partner with our PM to define the roadmap. Push back on scope when it's the right call.
- Build out our design system in collaboration with our existing senior designer. We use Figma and Tailwind.
- Lead user research sessions with developers. We talk to 4–6 customers a week.
- Help our DevRel team produce clear, well-designed documentation and onboarding flows.

What we're looking for

- 5+ years of product design experience. At least 2 years on a developer-facing product.
- Strong systems thinking. You can hold complexity in your head and ship simple interfaces.
- Comfort writing your own copy. Our voice is direct, technical, no fluff.
- Excellent written communication. We're a remote team that lives in long-form documents.
- Some frontend literacy. You don't need to ship production code, but you should be able to read and modify a Tailwind component.

What we offer

- Remote-first, headquartered in Berlin. Two retreats a year.
- Competitive salary, equity, generous PTO.
- A small team that ships fast and pushes back on each other constructively.
