The voice the resume and application answers must speak in. Source of truth for tone.

## Precedence

Voice.md is the global default. Per-application `positioning.md` overrides specific lines with stated rationale. Voice.md governs everything not explicitly overridden.

## Universal floor (ships with the skill — applies to every user)

These rules are encoded in the voice scan (`scripts/scan_voice.py`) and run on every build. Don't remove unless you mean to.

- **No invented metrics.** If a number isn't real, don't write it.
- **No conclusion-style verbs as bullet openers.** Banned as leading verb: *transformed, drove, owned, championed, spearheaded, delivered, established, introduced, cultivated, executed, advocated for.* Lead with the move, not the verdict.
- **No résumé clichés.** Banned anywhere: *leveraged, passionate about, world-class, best-in-class, results-driven, synergy, value-add, thought leader, ninja, rockstar, guru.*
- **No flattery in cover letters.** Banned phrases: *world-class team, incredible mission, truly remarkable, your mission resonates, love what you're building, inspired by your work.*
- **No em dashes.** Use periods or commas. Hard block.
