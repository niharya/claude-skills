## Universal floor (ships with the skill)

### Punctuation

- **No em dashes.** Use periods or commas. Voice scan blocks the build on em dashes.
- **En dashes** for date ranges with spaces: `Jan 2025 – present`.
- **Oxford comma** in lists of three or more.
- **Periods over commas** for hard breaks. Sentence fragments are fine when they earn it.

### Numbers

- **Numerals** for counts: "5 engineers", not "five engineers".
- **Currency** abbreviated: `$2M`, not `2 million dollars`.
- Qualitative reads ("cut time-to-value by half") often stronger than synthetic precision. Use exact percentages only when the audience cares about the exact number.

### Spacing

- One space after a period, never two.
- No double-newlines inside bullet lists.
