# Positioning: Northwind Cloud — Senior Product Designer — 2026-01-15

## Role-type

Dev Tools senior IC. Second designer joining an existing senior designer. Generalist — product, system, research, copy. Some frontend literacy expected.

## Audience

First read: the founding PM (technical, ex-engineer). Second read: the existing senior designer. They care about:

- Can this person hold complexity and ship simple interfaces.
- Have they actually worked on developer-facing products, or only consumer.
- Do they write well — the team lives in long-form docs.

Estimated read time: 3 minutes for the resume, 5 minutes for the cover letter.

## Screening test

Should read as: a designer who has shipped dev tools, can write, and can hold their own with a small senior team.

Should not read as: a generalist who hasn't worked on dev surfaces, or a strong design lead who needs to manage to be useful.

## Strategic frame

Lead with the dev-tools receipt. Push the consumer work later. Show frontend literacy without overclaiming engineering.
